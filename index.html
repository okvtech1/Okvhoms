Hotel software that works the way a front desk works

The OKV Hotel Management System runs on one computer in your hotel. Everyone else uses it through a web browser on the same Wi-Fi: the front desk, the manager's phone, the housekeeper's tablet. There is nothing to rent each month and no internet needed for daily work.

It covers the whole stay: reservations, the room calendar, check-in and check-out, charges and payments, housekeeping, expenses and reports. It is built for hotels, guest houses and lodges that want to stop using notebooks and spreadsheets without paying a monthly subscription.

It speaks your language of business: naira, VAT and service charge lines, bank transfer and POS payments, and a bill you can send to the guest on WhatsApp.

  • Made for small and mid-size properties
    From 5 rooms to a few hundred. No limit on rooms, guests or staff accounts.
  • Every role gets its own screen
    Administrator, front desk, accountant and housekeeping each see the numbers and buttons they need. You can create more roles.
  • Yours to customise
    Add your logo and colours, set your taxes and prices, and shape the forms to your hotel.
  • A manual for every role
    Each dashboard has a PDF user manual written for that job.

Runs on the computers and phones you already have

One computer acts as the server. Every other device simply opens a web address in its browser. No apps to install on phones.

Server computer

Windows 7, 8, 10 and 11, 32-bit or 64-bit, or a Mac (Intel or Apple chip). One quick installation.

Front desk PCs and laptops

Any computer with a browser: Windows, Mac, Chromebook or Linux. Open the address and sign in.

Phones and tablets

Android and iPhone or iPad on the same Wi-Fi. Scan the code on the server screen, then add it to the home screen like an app.

Printers

Print invoices, receipts and registration cards on A4, A5 or an 80 mm receipt roll.

How phones connect: the server shows an address such as http://192.168.1.20:8080 and a scan code. Type it or scan it on any phone connected to your hotel Wi-Fi. That is all.
