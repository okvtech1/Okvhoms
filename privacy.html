Privacy policy

Last updated 5 October 2026

Privacy policy Terms of service Cookie policy Refund policy Software licence Disclaimer

This policy explains how OKV Technology Consults ("we", "us") collects and uses personal data through this website and the OKV Hotel Management System ("the software"). We handle personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA).

1. What we collect

  • When you request the free trial: hotel name, your name, email, phone or WhatsApp number, city or state, number of rooms, and how you heard about us.
  • When you buy: the same contact details, the amount and date paid, your bank reference, and the payment receipt you upload (a photo or PDF, which may show account details). If you pay with Paystack, Paystack collects your card or bank details directly. We do not see or store them.
  • When you contact us or request installation: your message, location and preferred date.
  • From the software, only when you activate it: this computer's Machine ID, the software version and your hotel name.

2. What we do not collect

The software does not send us your guest records, bookings or payments. Those stay on your own computer. We see them only if you choose to give us a backup file for support.

3. Why we use your data, and our legal basis

  • To give you the trial, process your purchase, send activation codes and support you (performing our contract with you).
  • To prevent fraud and misuse of licences (our legitimate interest).
  • To keep financial and tax records (legal obligation).
  • To tell you about updates to the software you use, and to reply to your messages. You can ask us to stop marketing messages at any time.

4. Who handles your data

We use Google services (Sheets, Drive and Gmail) to store form entries, receipts and send email, and Paystack to take online payments. They process data for us under their own terms. Google's servers may be outside Nigeria, so your data can be transferred abroad. We do not sell your data.

5. How long we keep it

Trial and contact records are kept while they are useful for support and for up to 24 months after your last contact. Payment records, receipts and licence records are kept for as long as accounting and tax rules require, and for as long as your licence is active.

6. Security

Access to our records is limited to named staff with their own passwords. Receipts are stored in a private folder. Activation uses signed codes tied to one computer.

7. Your rights

You may ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its use, or move it. Email owner@okv.test. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

8. Hotels and their guests

If you run a hotel, you are the data controller for guest and staff information kept in the software. You decide what to collect and how long to keep it, and you must protect your computer and backups. We act only on your request when you ask for installation or support.

9. Children

Our website and software are for businesses and are not aimed at children.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above. Questions: owner@okv.test or WhatsApp +234 810 414 1138.
